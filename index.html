How to play

One color per tube

  1. Tap a tube to pick it up, then tap another to pour. You can also drag from one to the other.
  2. Liquid only pours onto the same color or into an empty tube.
  3. The whole top color pours at once, as much as fits.
  4. A tube is done when it's full of one color. It gets a cork.

Par is the move count for three stars. Hints and the extra tube each cost a star, undo is free.

Keyboard: 1–9 pick tubes, Z undo, R restart, H hint.
